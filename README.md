# Late Night

A tiny digital room for quiet evenings.

**[Open it →](https://ahsoka1tano.github.io/late-night/)**

Open it late, pick a mood, sit for a bit, close it.
No accounts, no backend, no notifications — just a dark little corner of the internet.

![The window seat: rain, a late city and a warm desk lamp](assets/window-seat.png)

## Choose an evening

The illustrated cards at the top set up a whole evening with one click:

- **Rainy records**: a lo-fi tape, real steady rain, the city and amber light.
- **Mountain hideaway**: a lo-fi tape, clear mountain skies and moon-coloured light.
- **The last light**: Sleep, distant recorded rain, mountains and a dim lamp.
- **Harbour after hours**: Calm, a real ocean recording, the coast, amber light and tea.
- **The last café**: a lo-fi tape, real light rain, coffee and a warm shop across the street.
- **Midnight garden**: a lo-fi tape, real drizzle, tea and a glowing greenhouse beside a pond.

Choosing a card starts its sounds. You can then change any dial; the selected
card loses its highlight when the setup no longer matches. Existing notes remain.

**Keep this evening** saves your current mood, view, weather, light and sound
choices on this device. **Your evening** restores that setup, including an off
lamp and a paused tape. Saving again replaces the saved setup. **Forget saved setup**
removes that shortcut without changing the room or stopping playback.
The selected tape is still managed separately by the tape shelf.

## A postcard from your window

Press **✉ postcard** above the window, including in Quiet room. A small workshop
captures an illustration of the current view using the room's own vectors:
city lights, mountains, the coast, the café or the midnight garden, with the
current sky, moon, rain and frame finish.

Add an optional caption of up to 80 characters, choose **night ink** or **moon
paper**, and decide whether to **include the time** beside the date. **Keep as
PNG** downloads a 1280 × 920 image. Nothing is uploaded or sent to a service.
The caption is plain text and stays in this open page; journal text is never
copied into a card automatically.

The view is captured when the workshop opens. Music, focus and window journeys
continue, while the card keeps that moment. **Capture this view again** takes
a fresh illustration without clearing your caption. Closing and reopening also
captures the latest view. Escape or the close button returns to the window;
keyboard focus stays inside the workshop while it is open.

![A downloaded postcard of the rainy city on moon paper](assets/window-postcard.png)

## The room around the view

Press **◇ room finish** above the window to try three materials:

- **Cedar**: long wooden grain, warm window rails and a broad wooden sill.
  It is the starting finish when no preference has been saved.
- **Midnight steel**: the original dark frame and crossed panes.
- **Orbital**: a rounded observation window, metal rim, small rivets and a
  low light along the hull. The horizontal crossbar leaves the view open.

Choices preview immediately. Keep the panel open to compare, close it with
**×**, or press Escape. Escape closes the finish panel before leaving Quiet
room; clicking outside also closes it. The control works in Quiet room and
on a phone, with scrolling available in short windows.

The finish stays independent of your mood and all five views. Tape, radio,
rain, focus sessions, journeys, glass sketches and lantern flights keep their
place. The lamp adds a faint edge reflection to Cedar and Orbital; switching
the lamp off removes that reflection. The orbital hull light rests in hidden
tabs and with reduced motion.

Your choice is remembered locally and included in **Your evening** and named
collected rooms, with matching small frames on their cards. Older saved rooms
keep the current material. Choosing a ready-made evening also keeps the finish
you chose. Storage failures leave the previous saved choice intact while the
new look works for this visit.

## A player within reach

The **Pocket player** stays at the bottom of the screen, including in **Quiet
room**. It follows the current tape, official radio or room recording, so you
can keep listening without scrolling back to the desk.

- **Play / pause** controls the tape, or the room recording in other moods.
  Pausing a tape leaves rain playing. **Next** follows your tape queue first.
- **≋** opens a small mixer with separate tape and room volume, an independent
  room-sound switch, and the existing real-rain switch in Music mood.
- **All quiet** stops tapes, room recordings and the radio. Your focus session,
  window journey and sound deadline keep their own state.

The mixer shows the full recording name. When a tape supplies a duration,
its position appears here too; seeking while paused keeps it paused. The
ordinary desk controls and the Pocket player share the same audio and levels.
Loading, unavailable recordings and muted levels have their own labels.

YouTube keeps its own transport and volume controls. **Radio ↗** takes you to
the existing embedded player, leaving Quiet room if necessary. The Pocket
player reports whether that player is open; it does not guess whether YouTube
is currently audible. Opening the controls preserves an existing iframe.

**Minimize player** inside the mixer folds it into a small title button. Click
that button to expand it. Resizing keeps playback unchanged, and the layout is
remembered on this device. Remembering the layout does not start any audio.
Escape closes the mixer first; another Escape can then leave Quiet room.
Clicking outside also closes the mixer. Reels rest in hidden tabs and with
reduced motion. Narrow screens and short landscape windows can scroll the mixer.

## Let the window wander

Under the mood buttons, choose a **Night journey**. The illustrated routes move
only the view, keeping your current tape or radio, rain, light and desk objects.

- **After hours**: City → Café → Garden.
- **Far away**: Mountains → Coast → Garden.
- **The long way**: City → Café → Garden → Mountains → Coast.

Choose **30 sec**, **1 min** or **3 min** at each window, then press a route card.
You arrive at the window immediately. If your current view is on that route,
the journey begins there; otherwise it starts at the first stop. Routes loop.
A small ticket on the glass shows your current stop and a gentle progress line.

**Pause** stays at the current view and keeps the unspent time. **Next view** or
a numbered stop changes the view immediately, even while paused. **Stay here**
ends the route without moving you. The compact controls above the window also
work in **Quiet room**. Choosing a view yourself or opening a saved evening
ends the journey so your choice can stay.

Hidden tabs wait at their current stop; returning resumes the remaining time,
without skipping through places you missed. Reduced motion keeps the progress
line still and removes animated scene transitions. Your last route and pace are
remembered locally, but a reload never starts a journey automatically. Saved
room setups keep the current view, with no running journey attached. Focus
sessions, journal entries, the tape queue and sound deadlines stay independent.

## A collection of your own evenings

Open **Your collected evenings**, just below the ready-made cards. Settle the
room, give it a name and press **collect this room**. Up to eight named setups
can stay on this device, each with a small illustration of its view, sky and
curtains. Collecting and renaming do not change the current room or its playback.

Open a collected card to restore its mood, view, rain, ambient choice, lighting,
drink, cat and curtains, including saved off and paused states. A room saved with
active sound starts that sound when opened. As with the original quick save,
the selected cassette and volume settings remain managed by the player; focus
sessions, sound deadlines and journal notes continue independently.

**Rename** changes only the name. **Remove** makes space; **undo removal** brings
back the most recently removed card during this visit, unless all eight places
have since been filled. Names are plain text and can be up to 40 characters.
The original **keep this evening** shortcut remains separate from the collection.

The collection lives in localStorage, with no account or upload. If saving fails,
the previous collection and the name draft stay in place. Unreadable data is left
untouched; reopening retries the read. There is no cloud backup.

## Draw on the glass

In **Draw on the glass**, press **start drawing**. A veil of mist settles over
the window; trace through it with a mouse, finger or pen to reveal the view.
Choose **fingertip** or **a little wider** for the next stroke. The brush width
is remembered on this device; the drawing itself is never saved or sent.

**Pause drawing** leaves the marks in place and lets you use the window normally.
Escape also pauses drawing. **Mist over again** starts a fresh misted pane;
**let it clear** removes the mist and every mark. Reloading also clears drawings.

For a small ready-made trace, use **trace a heart** or **a small moon**. These
buttons work with the keyboard and bring the window into view. The **♡** button
beside the window traces a heart directly, including in **Quiet room**; it becomes
**×** to clear the glass without leaving the window.

Drawings keep their proportions when the window changes size. An interrupted
touch or a hidden tab releases the gesture; painting only runs when the glass
changes. Views, curtains, music, real rain, focus sessions and journal notes
continue independently. Saved evenings keep room settings, never the marks.

## Let it drift

**Quiet room**, with Enter or Space.

Up to three lanterns can drift at once. Each passes in 30 seconds; **clear the
sky** removes them sooner. Notes exist only for their flight: they are never
saved or sent. The colour is remembered on this device and included in your
saved evenings, without saving notes or launching lanterns when restored.

Music, rain and focus continue independently. Hidden tabs pause the animation
and remove expired flights on return. With reduced motion, lanterns stay still
before disappearing.

## A few records for later

**Up next** sits below the tape player. Add the current recording with **＋ this
tape**, or open the cassette shelf and use **＋ up next** under any recording.
Up to eight different tapes can wait in order. Adding a tape does not start or
replace the current recording.

Use **↑** and **↓** to change the order. **×** removes one upcoming tape;
**undo removal** brings back the last removed item during this visit, if there
is still room. **Clear queue** removes the upcoming list without stopping music.
All actions work with the keyboard and have names that include the recording.

**Cue next** and the player's **next** button choose the first available queued
recording. Playing stays playing; paused stays paused. When a playing tape ends,
the next queued recording follows automatically. Real rain keeps playing through
the change. When the queue is empty, Next follows the regular shelf and its saved
tapes filter. Radio channels keep their own player and do not consume the queue.

The upcoming order is remembered on this device, separately from saved room setups.
Reloading restores the list without starting a tape. A recording missing from the
current catalogue stays labelled in the list and is skipped for playback; you can
remove it or wait for the catalogue to return. Queue entries only play recordings
already in the curated shelf.

If saving fails, edits work for this visit and the previously saved order remains.
Unreadable stored data is left untouched; reload retries the read. A failed audio
load offers the existing retry controls rather than silently consuming more tapes.

## Somewhere else: a shelf of sound

In the room controls, **Somewhere else** has four illustrated recording cards:
**Rain**, **Ocean**, **Fireplace** and **Space**. Choose one, then press **listen**.
Choosing a card while paused only cues the sound. While listening, it switches
the background without restarting a tape or the official radio player.

The chosen recording stays with you across moods, independently of the view and
visible weather. Rain has the same drizzle / steady / downpour dial even under
a clear sky. The room volume and sound timer apply to every background.
**Follow the mood** returns to the original mood-based recordings.

The window button starts lo-fi together with your chosen background; it keeps
clear weather clear. With **follow the mood**, it starts the original lo-fi and
rain evening. **Add real rain** returns to that mood-based rain setup.

Your sound choice is remembered on this device and included in **Keep this
evening**. Ready-made cards restore their own original sound setups. An older
saved evening still works and follows its mood. Each recording has a source and
credits link; loading failures offer **retry**. Rain, ocean and fireplace stream
from the Internet Archive; Space uses the existing local CC0 recording.

## Let the sound end softly

**A little longer**, in the room controls, sets a **15, 30 or 60 minute** sound
timer. It starts no playback. In the final minute, tapes and ambient recordings
fade together without changing your volume settings. The official radio stops
at the end; its embedded player does not fade.

The small moon countdown at the window stays available in **Quiet room**. Touch
it to cancel and keep listening at your chosen volume. **Turn off the desk lamp
at the end** is optional and starts unchecked. The focus timer and journal continue
independently; stopping the sound does not finish a focus session.

The deadline and lamp choice stay on this device. Refreshing keeps the original
end time. An overdue timer stops remembered ambience before it can start, and
the finished state never starts another recording. Suspended tabs catch up as
soon as the browser resumes; the timer uses elapsed wall time.

## A quiet minute

Choose **take a breath** above the window for a short rhythm of light on the glass.
It works with any mood: your lo-fi, rain, radio and focus timer keep going. The
same controls remain at the window in **Quiet room**. Pause or leave whenever you like.

In **Calm**, the desk offers **1, 3 or 5 minutes** and two rhythms: **gentle**
(four seconds in, six out) or **even** (four in, four out). Duration and pace stay
on this device. Reset the current ritual before changing them. Refreshing the
page starts no ritual automatically; there are no streaks or focus points for this.

Leaving the tab pauses the rhythm, and returning waits for **continue**. Reduced
motion keeps the light still while the phase text continues. Screen readers get
one announcement per phase. Sleep waits until the rhythm finishes before dimming
the room again. At the end, nothing else starts: just a little space to sit.

## Lo-fi + rain

In Tape, **pick a tape for tonight** lets you choose a recording by name. The room
remembers your selection. Choosing another tape while playing changes only the
music; choosing one while paused leaves it paused. Rain and volume stay as you set them.
Press **♡ keep** to save a tape on this device. **Saved tapes** narrows the list,
and **next** then cycles through those recordings. Saving and filtering never start
playback. Remove the final saved tape to return to the full collection.

Choose **Music**, press **play** on the tape (or start a Lofi Girl radio), then
**add real rain**. Music and weather have independent volume sliders. Drizzle,
steady rain and downpour work here too; changing weather leaves the music alone.
Your weather choice is remembered. Browsers may require **listen** after a reload.
If a rain recording is unavailable, **retry** appears; rain is never replaced by
generated noise. The recordings need an internet connection.

## Running it

Before publishing changed CSS, JavaScript or the train artwork, run
`node scripts/refresh-assets.cjs` and commit the updated `index.html`. The URLs
include a content fingerprint so browsers fetch the matching assets after an update.
The site still runs directly, without a build step.

No build step. Open `index.html`, or serve the folder:

```bash
python -m http.server 5173
```

Then visit <http://localhost:5173>.

## Journal

Write up to 140 characters, including line breaks. Each edit saves the note and
emotion together on this device. If saving fails, the draft stays in the editor
with a visible message; editing again or leaving the field retries the save.
Unreadable stored notes are never silently replaced with an empty journal.

When you return to the tab after midnight, the editor opens the new day once the
previous draft is saved. A note being written across midnight stays with the
evening it began. Localhost and the published site have separate journals.

Open **previous nights** below the editor to read older notes and emotions.
The newest evenings appear first, seven at a time; **older nights** reveals the
next seven. Today's draft stays in the editor. Existing notes use their original
local dates, and browsing the history never edits them.

## Browser checks (optional)

These checks use isolated browser profiles and sample notes. They cover immediate
reload, fast emotion changes, storage failures, midnight, history ordering and
pagination, safe rendering of note text, and mobile and desktop layouts.
The website itself needs no dependencies or build step.

```bash
npm install --no-save --package-lock=false playwright
npx playwright install chromium
node --test checks/journal.test.cjs checks/atmosphere.test.cjs
```

To use an installed Edge instead, set `PLAYWRIGHT_CHANNEL=msedge`. A separately
installed Playwright can be selected with `PLAYWRIGHT_MODULE` (its module path).

Atmosphere checks cover simultaneous layers, independent volume, recording errors,
weather persistence, the unified room, keyboard access, radio continuity and layouts
at 320, 844 and 1440 pixels. They use a deterministic media double, so they do not
prove availability or audibility of external recordings. Set `ATMOSPHERE_PREVIEW_DIR`
to an existing folder to save screenshots.

The radio side is [Lofi Girl](https://www.youtube.com/@LofiGirl)'s own live streams —
lofi hip hop, lofi house, synthwave, summer lofi and deep sleep — embedded with
YouTube's own player. Her stream ids change when she restarts a broadcast.

The space ambience is [Floating in Space](https://opengameart.org/content/floating-in-space) by Umplix (CC0), stored in the project so playback does not depend on OpenGameArt's file server.

## Stack

HTML, CSS, vanilla JS. On purpose.

Nothing leaves the browser: the mood, the journal and the settings live in `localStorage`,
there is no account, no server and nothing to sign up for.
