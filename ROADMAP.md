# Roadmap

Not a plan, more of a direction. One small thing per evening.

**The heart of it: lo-fi and rain.** Everything else is decoration around a dark window,
a slow beat and water running down the glass. When in doubt — make the rain nicer.

## Rain (the main thread)
- [ ] animated rain layer: drops falling over the whole page
- [ ] drops sliding down the "glass", with trails
- [ ] blurred night-city lights behind the window (bokeh)
- [ ] rain intensity: drizzle / steady / downpour
- [ ] occasional distant lightning, very rare, very soft
- [ ] rain sound loop, fading in with the visuals
- [ ] window fog that slowly gathers at the edges

## Lo-fi
- [ ] lo-fi player bar: play / pause, track title, thin progress line
- [ ] a small local loop or a chosen stream, whatever feels best
- [ ] tape hiss / vinyl crackle layer on top of anything playing
- [ ] separate volume for music and rain — mix your own night
- [ ] slow cassette-style animation while playing

## Foundations
- [x] night screen, live clock, typography
- [x] mood selector with six atmospheres
- [ ] smoother mood transitions, per-mood background details
- [ ] keyboard shortcuts (1–6 for moods, space for play)

## Focus
- [ ] 25:00 timer with start / pause / reset
- [ ] duration presets: 15 / 25 / 45 / 60
- [ ] quiet finish state instead of an alarm
- [ ] rain keeps going while you focus

## Ambient (beyond rain)
- [ ] ambient cards: Ocean, Fireplace, Space, Cafe, Night City
- [ ] local audio loops with soft fade in/out

## Evening rituals
- [ ] daily vibe line, one per night
- [ ] tiny journal: a mood chip and one sentence
- [ ] everything stored in localStorage

## Traces
- [ ] nights visited, focus sessions, minutes focused
- [ ] gentle streak counter

## Always
- polish, animation, mobile, micro-interactions
- new atmospheres and phrases
- the occasional easter egg
